## Bottom-line classification

`(a)` is definitional/too-elementary-to-cite. `(b)`'s **existence** as "some" asymmetric tightening of the general symmetric bound is **very likely already published** (Harker et al. 2019, cited by a careful, recent, arXiv-verified source as precisely the reference for this question) — this pass could not independently confirm the exact match, and following the task's mandated aggressive-skepticism default, **the honest classification is "probable specialized corollary, not confirmed novel."** The genuinely new, independently-verified contributions of this pass are: (i) the from-scratch elementary derivation avoiding heavier machinery, and (ii) the **sharpness proof** (`H0_SHARPNESS.md`) — no source found or cited anywhere in this search establishes or discusses sharpness of the constant for this specific asymmetric setting.
